# Lannkz YouTube Content Performance Analysis — Findings

## Research Question

**How are football relevance and release relevance associated with click-through rate (CTR), viewer retention, and engaged views on Lannkz?**

## Dataset and Scope

The dataset contains 58 YouTube videos.

- **Full historical dataset:** 58 videos.
- **Primary analysis:** 53 manga-focused videos published after February 2, 2025.
- **Early anime-era videos:** Five videos published on or before February 2, 2025.
- **FC25 sensitivity analysis:** Two unusually successful creative-format videos are retained in the data but can be excluded to assess their influence.

The Power BI report defaults to the manga-focused era. Historical and sensitivity comparisons are explicitly identified below.

Football relevance and release relevance are manually assigned categorical variables. Their associations with performance are descriptive rather than causal.

## Primary Findings — Manga-Focused Era (53 Videos)

### Finding 1: Football-Focused Videos Show Relatively Strong Viewer Retention

**Evidence**

High-football-relevance videos averaged approximately 42–45% viewer retention across the available release-relevance groups.

Higher football relevance did not consistently correspond to higher CTR.

**Interpretation**

Football-focused analysis appears capable of retaining viewers effectively, but it does not necessarily generate stronger initial click-through performance.

**Recommendation**

Continue producing football-focused analysis while experimenting with titles, thumbnails, and topic framing to improve audience reach.

### Finding 2: Release Relevance Does Not Consistently Predict Higher CTR

**Evidence**

Within the medium-football-relevance category, average CTR increased modestly across release-relevance levels, from approximately 5.02% to 5.36%.

The pattern was not consistent across all football-relevance categories.

**Interpretation**

Publishing content related to recent manga developments is not consistently associated with higher CTR.

**Recommendation**

Prioritize timely topics when they support a compelling analytical idea rather than relying on recency alone.

### Finding 3: The Highest Median Viewership Combination Has a Small Sample

**Evidence**

Medium-football/high-release videos recorded approximately 67,266 median engaged views.

However, this subgroup contained only two videos.

**Interpretation**

The result identifies a successful combination within the observed dataset, but the sample is too small to establish a reliable performance expectation.

**Recommendation**

Avoid treating small subgroup rankings as predictive rules for future uploads.

## Sensitivity Analysis

### 1. Publishing Era

The five early anime-era videos were excluded to evaluate whether the channel's earlier content strategy influenced overall performance.

| Metric | All 58 Videos | Manga-Focused 53 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average CTR | 4.93% | 4.88% |
| Average retention | 42.47% | 42.79% |
| Average engaged views | 48.93K | 36.41K |

Excluding the five early videos reduced average engaged views by approximately **25.6%**, while CTR and retention changed relatively little.

**Interpretation**

Early uploads disproportionately influence the channel's historical average viewership. The manga-focused dataset provides a more relevant descriptive benchmark for the channel's current content strategy.

This does not establish that anime-related demand caused the performance difference.

### 2. FC25 Creative-Format Videos

Two unusually successful FC25 player-card videos are classified as General Analysis with medium football relevance.

Excluding them reduced median engaged views for the medium-football/low-release group from approximately **33,125 to 24,020**, a **27.5% decrease**.

The videos are retained in the original dataset because their performance is legitimate, but this sensitivity test demonstrates that some subgroup findings depend on exceptional uploads.

**Interpretation**

Content-format differences and individual breakout videos can influence apparent relationships between football relevance and viewership.

## Historical Supporting Analysis — Full 58 Videos

The following results describe the complete historical dataset and should not be interpreted as findings from the 53-video primary sample.

### Finding 4: Scouting Reports Show Strong Typical Performance

- Scouting Reports analyzed: 21
- Median engaged views: 30,175
- Average retention: 44.24%
- Average CTR: 4.96%

Scouting Reports had the highest median engaged views and average retention among the five content categories in the historical dataset.

**Recommendation:** Maintain Scouting Reports as a core content format while considering character demand, production costs, and exposure.

### Finding 5: Longer Videos Generate More Watch Time per View

| Duration | Videos | Average retention | Average view duration |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Under 10 minutes | 27 | 45.13% | 3.60 min |
| 10–15 minutes | 25 | 41.97% | 4.88 min |
| 15+ minutes | 6 | 32.57% | 6.40 min |

Longer videos had lower percentage retention but higher average view duration.

**Recommendation:** Choose video length according to topic depth rather than maximizing percentage retention alone.

### Finding 6: CTR–Retention Relationships Vary by Content Type

Historical correlations between CTR and average percentage viewed:

| Content type | Videos | Correlation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Scouting Report | 21 | +0.309 |
| General Analysis | 14 | −0.627 |
| Tactical Analysis | 12 | −0.535 |
| Match Analysis | 9 | +0.116 |
| Theory / Prediction | 2 | −1.000 |

The relationship varies across content types, although sample sizes and confounding variables limit interpretation.

The two-video Theory / Prediction correlation is not meaningful for generalization.

**Recommendation:** Investigate high-CTR, low-retention videos for possible differences between viewer expectations and delivered content, without assuming that packaging causes retention changes.

### Finding 7: Timely Videos Produce Breakout Hits, but Typical Performance Is Closer

Historical release-relevance comparisons:

| Release relevance | Videos | Mean engaged views | Median engaged views |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Low | 40 | 44,426 | 18,777 |
| Medium | 6 | 25,187 | 21,848.5 |
| High | 12 | 75,812 | 22,800 |

High-release-relevance videos had substantially higher mean engaged views than low-release-relevance videos, but the difference between their medians was much smaller.

This suggests that individual successful uploads contribute disproportionately to the difference in means.

**Recommendation:** Balance timely and evergreen videos rather than assuming every upload must follow the latest manga development.

## Overall Recommendations

1. Benchmark current uploads against comparable manga-focused videos.
2. Continue football-focused analysis while testing improvements to topic framing and packaging.
3. Evaluate impressions alongside CTR and retention when diagnosing limited viewership.
4. Consider content type, publishing era, and subgroup size before interpreting performance differences.
5. Treat exceptional uploads as useful case studies rather than guaranteed templates for future success.

## Limitations

- Observational data from a single YouTube channel.
- Small sample sizes in several relevance combinations.
- Manually assigned football and release relevance categories.
- Overlap between content type, relevance, and publishing era.
- Differences in video age, audience demand, and recommendation exposure.
- Lifetime video metrics rather than equal post-publication observation windows.
- CTR and retention do not fully explain impression distribution.
- Correlations and subgroup differences do not establish causation.
- YouTube's view-counting methodology changed in August 2026; engaged views were used for more consistent comparisons.

## Conclusion

The analysis suggests that football-focused content can achieve relatively strong viewer retention, but higher football relevance and topical timeliness do not consistently correspond to stronger CTR or viewership.

Publishing-era and FC25 sensitivity checks demonstrate that historical context and exceptional videos materially influence some performance comparisons.

For Lannkz, the most defensible strategy is to maintain its analytical content identity while evaluating new uploads against relevant benchmarks and using impressions, CTR, retention, and engaged views together when making content decisions.
